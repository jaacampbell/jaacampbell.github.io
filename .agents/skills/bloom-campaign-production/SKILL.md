# Bloom Campaign Production
Activate on a branded multi-image campaign. Read current connected brand guidance first. Determine the campaign objective, audiences, exact approved copy, formats, safe areas and deliverable count. Create a coherent editorial visual language but give each image a distinct story and composition. Produce separate assets as separate files; do not substitute one grid for a requested batch. Preserve current approved ticket prices, sponsor promises and affiliate terms. Never fabricate scarcity or make up brand marks. Check image fidelity and text. If the user says Go, execute established direction rather than re-asking answered questions. Respect image-generation credits and do not repeatedly generate unnecessary variants.
